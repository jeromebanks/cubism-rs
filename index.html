The design in 30 seconds

Delegate an outcome.
Keep authority over the result.

Nightshift is a governed software delivery service for work that outlasts one coding session. Its product is reliable delegation with inspectable evidence.

Human direction

Agree on the contract

Define scope, acceptance criteria, risk, budget, and the next milestone. Deployment authority is granted explicitly.

Bounded autonomy

Let delivery continue

Agents plan and implement small slices. The platform schedules work, enforces limits, commissions review, and recovers from routine failures.

Verifiable outcomes

Review what was achieved

See the integrated demo, source and test evidence, important decisions, and known gaps. Accept an exact, immutable evidence bundle.

  1. AuthorizeOutcome → milestone contract → dependency graph.

  3. VerifyCold review → integration checks → governed merge.

Routine passing PRs merge autonomously. Human attention is reserved for product milestones and decisions outside the delegated authority.

  • Applied merge raises a NameError.
  • Self-authored review receipts and pass-then-fail histories can satisfy the gate.
  • Conflicting check names collapse to the last result.
  • Human-gate and feedback exceptions are incompletely enforced.
  • Branch-based claims lack exclusive ownership, leases, and fencing.

Recommended reference architecture

Durable state at the center.
Separate authority from execution.

The platform owns contracts and workflow state. Source hosts own Git facts. Customer CI remains usable; adapters connect each system without pretending they share one transaction.

Control plane

Contracts, dependency graph, scheduler, leases, budget reservations, policy decisions, durable workflows, and live cockpit.

Execution plane

Ephemeral VMs for implementation, independent review, tests, builds, and demos. Qualified adapters support multiple harnesses and models.

Evidence plane

Content-addressed artifacts, role-specific attestations, immutable milestone bundles, human decisions, and audit exports.

Privileged boundary: policy activation, signing, source writes, and deployment run through separately authorized services. Coding agents receive scoped capabilities.

The non-negotiable controls

Trust is earned at each transition.

  • One authoritative ownerTransactional leases, heartbeats, generations, and fencing reject new effects from stale attempts.
  • Independent review identitiesSeparate assignments, environments, credentials, and context. A different model name alone does not establish independence.
  • Immutable evidence subjectsReviews, tests, builds, and demos bind to exact source and artifact digests. A new candidate requires current evidence.
  • Policy cannot approve itselfSigned policy is activated outside the candidate PR. Agents cannot grant permissions or weaken their own gate.
  • Ambiguity remains visibleExternal effects have durable intent and reconciliation. Unknown merge or deployment outcomes block unsafe retries.
  • Budgets include failed workReserve before launch; account for review, retries, abandoned attempts, CI, infrastructure, and outstanding exposure.

The human milestone contract

An acceptance decision you can defend.

The factory presents exactly what was included, what was excluded, what passed, what remains uncertain, and what your decision authorizes next. Your sign-off is bound to the evidence bundle you reviewed.

Changed source, artifacts, material configuration, or acceptance criteria require new evidence and a new bundle. Acceptance never silently transfers to changed work.

Five stages, demonstrated at each checkpoint

Earn the next level of autonomy.

Retain the prototype’s useful assets. Each stage is divided into independently reviewable vertical slices, with acceptance criteria, a demonstration, risks, non-goals, and exit or kill conditions.

STAGE 0Trustworthy Cubism dogfoodRepair and establish honest limits

Repair merge, receipt, readiness, feedback, and claim controls. Keep a single trusted dispatcher while the concurrency guarantee is local.

Demonstrate: a small correction through independent review, merge, evidence, and milestone acceptance. Known adversarial cases must fail closed.

Checkpoint: accept bounded dogfood under its stated single-host trust assumptions.

STAGE 1Single-repository kernelMake state and recovery durable

Extract versioned contracts, PostgreSQL authority, leases, checkpoints, authenticated review, and the human decision service. Existing scripts become clients.

Demonstrate: kill the orchestrator between publication and result recording; restart without duplicate PRs or lost state.

Checkpoint: accept the domain model, evidence contract, and recovery experience.

STAGE 2Hosted multi-runner pilotProve value across agents

Introduce isolated hosted runners, two qualified harnesses, a separate review pool, protected integration, and a hosted demo.

Demonstrate: run a dependency graph, lose a runner and a provider, then complete the milestone without lowering assurance.

Checkpoint: a pilot customer accepts usefulness, cost, evidence clarity, and interruption rate.

STAGE 3Multi-tenant betaQualify isolation and operations

Enforce tenant boundaries, quotas, billing, privacy, export/deletion, access revocation, and disaster recovery.

Demonstrate: run two tenants; stop one while the other continues. Reject cross-tenant access and restore with a new authority epoch.

Checkpoint: independent isolation assessment and product/security acceptance for limited beta.

STAGE 4Enterprise platformExtend governance and deployment

Support customer runners and keys, delegated approvers, multi-repository release vectors, regional failover, and independently verifiable audit exports.

Demonstrate: accept a multi-repository milestone after a partial deployment failure and governed recovery.

Checkpoint: enterprise operational readiness, contracted SLO evidence, and sustainable support boundaries.

The first proof should be one complete milestone.

Include an injected failure, independent review, a verified demonstration, and human acceptance bound to the exact evidence. Advance only when the operating model works under interruption.
